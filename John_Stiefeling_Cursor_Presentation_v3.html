01 — HIRING FOR THE AWKWARD TEENAGE YEARS
How I hire
Four-component screen
Hiring coach + standardized prep — same starting conditions for every candidate. Evaluating talent, not preparation access.
Customer story presentation — limbic grabber, north star in 60 sec, 10-K research required, customer discovery voice, outcomes not features, Why Change / Why Now / Why Cursor.
Discovery practice session — live, unscripted. I play the skeptical executive. Do they active listen? Follow the customer's thread? Find the emotional undercurrent?
48-hour build challenge — "Build something in Cursor you'd show a VP Eng at a Fortune 500."
Scaling without diluting quality
The bar doesn't change as we scale — the screen does. Peer review baked in from Day 1. The team owns the bar, not just me.
How I retain
Meaning, not money.
Everyone must answer: "What did I build this quarter that didn't exist before?"
Early signal I watch for: energy, not performance. Unsolicited ideas stop. Push-back stops. That's the signal.
Context before capability — most underperformers are misdeployed. Fix the context first. One behavior at a time, same-day feedback.
Prep PIP before formal PIP — no surprises. The honest conversation happens before the process, not during it.
Growth paths aren't a ladder. They're a vector. I tell every person where I see them going — then get out of the way.
