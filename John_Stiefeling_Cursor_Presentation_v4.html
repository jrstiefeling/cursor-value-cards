02 — THE CULTURE: NOTHING BELOW YOU
Performance reviews — three layers
If someone genuinely understands why their work matters — the right behavior follows. You don't need to manage people who see the why.
Personal — proud of / wish I'd done differently / where next. Pride first. Direction third.
Multiplier audit — what did you teach, build, create, learn? Equal weight to metrics. An SE who hit numbers but kept everything in their head is not a high performer at Cursor.
Strategic alignment — can you draw the line from your work to Cursor's $2B→$6B trajectory? What's your 1–3 year plan and how does achieving it help us get there?
Goal-setting at the start of every quarter
"You want enterprise leadership in 2 years. That means you own a $1M+ deal end to end this year. Which means a named economic buyer in active evaluation by Q2. Your goal and Cursor's goal are the same goal. Let's build the plan." That's compliance vs. conviction.
Recognition as a system
I recognize the behavior I want more of — not just the outcome. The invisible multiplier behaviors go unnoticed and eventually stop happening.
Specific and public — "The way you reframed that CISO objection — turning data residency from a blocker into a proof point — that's the move I want every SE to make." Specificity teaches the whole team.
Kudos channel · SE-nominated awards · Slack wow emoji creator — lateral recognition, peer judgment, native celebration
Recognition during hard times — the deal that didn't close but where the SE ran their best discovery session. Effort and resilience named. Keeps the team swinging at hard problems instead of retreating to safe ones.
The proof point
Coach of the Year — Slack + Tableau simultaneous integrations, no playbook, team that had never read a 10-K. Built the motion with them, not for them.
